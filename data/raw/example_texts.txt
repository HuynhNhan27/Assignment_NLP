Example educational texts for testing

=== Example 1: Simple Food Chain ===
Cows are herbivorous mammals that eat grass in meadows.
They are larger than sheep, which also eat grass and leaves.
Both cows and deer consume plant matter like grass and leaves as food.

=== Example 2: Animal Classification ===
A cat is a small carnivorous mammal with fur, whiskers, and four legs.
Dogs are also mammals, domesticated for hunting and companionship.
Birds like eagles are carnivorous animals that hunt smaller creatures.

=== Example 3: Ecosystem ===
In a forest ecosystem, trees provide shelter and food for animals.
Rabbits eat clover and grass, while foxes hunt rabbits.
Decomposers like fungi break down dead organic matter.
